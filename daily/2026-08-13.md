### 1. 断言写不好，等于给自己埋定时炸弹

- **是什么**：`assert 条件` 是「我认为这里一定成立，不成立就报错」的自检。
- **为什么今天用**：你昨天写的 `assert read_back[2]["temperature"] == "36.8"` 是坏的——36.8 是**实时温度**，广州明天 36.9 就炸。断言只该写「不变的事实」。
- **怎么用**：写「不变的事实」断言：

  ```python
  rows = list(reader)
  assert len(rows) == 3            # 有效城市固定 3 个
  for r in rows:
      t = float(r["temperature"])  # 能转成数字
      assert -50 < t < 60          # 在地球温度范围内
  ```

- **坑**：把 API 返回值、当前时间、随机数这些「每次跑都变」的东西写进断言，测试就变成彩票。

### 2. 坏行处理要「坏在哪、跳在哪」

- **是什么**：CSV 解析的坏行跳过，必须在**第一次发现坏字段的地方就 continue**，别想着后面再拦。
- **为什么今天用**：你昨天 load_cities 里纬度转换失败只 print 没 continue——「月球」那行是经度也坏才被跳的，运气好。如果一行是 `月球,abc,116.4`（纬度坏、经度好），它会被当合法城市混进结果，主流程 fetch 时再炸。
- **怎么用**：

  ```python
  try:
      lat = float(list1[1])
  except ValueError:
      print(f"第{i}行跳过：{list1[0]} 纬度不是数字")
      continue          # ← 发现坏就跳，别继续往下
  try:
      lon = float(list1[2])
  except ValueError:
      print(f"第{i}行跳过：{list1[0]} 经度不是数字")
      continue
  ```

- **坑**：错误信息要和字段对得上（你昨天把纬度打成了 temperature，排查时误导人）。

### 3. 字典映射表：查表用 `get`，别用 `[k]`

- **是什么**：`{"数字": "描述"}` 一张映射表，查表用 `table.get(code, 默认值)`。
- **为什么今天用**：weathercode 是数字，人看不懂（53 是什么？）。映射成「毛毛雨」「阴」才有用；`get` 的第二个参数给默认值，查不到的 code 不崩。
- **怎么用**：

  ```python
  WEATHER = {0: "晴", 2: "多云", 3: "阴", 53: "毛毛雨", 61: "小雨", 80: "阵雨"}
  desc = WEATHER.get(code, f"未知代码{code}")   # 查不到也优雅兜底
  ```

- **坑**：`WEATHER[code]` 查不到直接 KeyError 崩掉整个程序。以后做用户输入、API 返回值的查表，一律 `get`。

### 4. 认识 csv 模块的 DictReader（今天不改代码，认识即可）

- **是什么**：`csv.DictReader(f)` 按表头自动把每行转成 dict，不用手拆字符串、不用手动跳过表头。
- **为什么今天用**：你 save_results 的 DictWriter 用对了；load_cities 还在手拆。第 3 周处理真数据（上千行）时手拆必出 bug，先认识正规写法。
- **怎么用**：

  ```python
  import csv
  with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as f:
      for row in csv.DictReader(f):   # row 直接是 dict，表头自动跳过
          print(row["city"], row["latitude"])
  ```

- **坑**：DictReader 不会帮你做类型转换——读进来全是字符串，坏纬度照样要自己 try/continue（预习第 2 条的逻辑不变）。

Anna wanted to learn the piano. On her first day, her teacher said, "The most important thing is not talent. It is patience."

For two months, Anna practiced every evening. She played the same five notes again and again, until her fingers hurt. After two months, she could play the whole piece — but slowly, and with many mistakes. She was not happy.

"I have practiced for two months," she told her teacher. "I still play badly. Maybe I should give up. My friend Lily started at the same time, and she can already play two pieces."

"You cannot see a tree grow. If you watch it for one minute, nothing changes. But if you come back after one year, it is taller than your head. Progress is like that. Do not compare yourself with Lily. Compare yourself with the Anna of yesterday."

Anna listened. She did not give up. She kept practicing, day after day, even when she made the same mistake three times in a row.

Six months later, she played the piece without mistakes. One year later, she played it with her eyes closed, and her teacher smiled for the first time.

Later, Anna taught her own students. She always told them the same thing: "Progress is quiet. You cannot see it today. But if you look back after one month, you will be surprised how far you have come."
